When two transactions were done within 2 minutes of each other, you choose which ones really went through. This keeps your cash and float correct when you had to redo a failed transaction.

On: numbers up to 2 digits different, similar amounts, and different jobs (for example a Deposit and then a Float to the same person) count as repeats. Off: only the same job, number and amount count.

Wait before asking questions
After you open the app or come back from dialing, the app waits this long, and until you stop tapping, before asking "Did it go through?" or about repeated transactions. This way it does not interrupt you while you are busy.
